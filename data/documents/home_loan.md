## Home loan application

Applicants should submit a completed application, identity proof, current
address proof, PAN or applicable tax identification, recent salary slips or
business income records, bank statements for the previous six months, and
property title and sale documents. Self-employed applicants should also submit
the latest two years of tax returns and audited financial statements when
available.

For this sample bank, the indicative home-loan tenure is up to 30 years. Final
approval, rates, required documents, and loan amount depend on underwriting and
the current product offer. Customers should confirm current terms with the bank.
