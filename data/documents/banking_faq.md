## Everyday banking questions

The sample savings account requires a minimum average monthly balance of
₹5,000. Fees may apply when the balance requirement is not met; the applicable
fee depends on the account variant. Contact the bank to confirm the current
minimum balance and fees for a specific account.

EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment. It is a fixed periodic payment toward a
loan, generally covering both principal and interest. The payment amount can
depend on principal, interest rate, and repayment tenure. A missed EMI can
result in late fees, additional interest, and a negative effect on credit
history, according to the loan agreement and applicable rules.
