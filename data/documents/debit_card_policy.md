## Lost or stolen debit card

If a debit card is lost or stolen, block it immediately using the bank's mobile
application or internet banking, or call the customer support number printed on
the official bank website or account statement. Never share your PIN, password,
or one-time passcode with anyone. Report transactions you do not recognize to
the bank promptly. This sample does not contain a real bank telephone number.
